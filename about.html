Our Team

Our team brings together decades of experience in tea brokerage, auction operations, specialty teas, finance, and client relations — ensuring excellence at every stage of the tea trade.

Managing Director

A seasoned tea management professional with over 22 years in Kenya’s tea sector. Experienced in tea tasting, judging, factory management, HR services, governance, and marketing. Expert in Black CTC, Orthodox, and Specialty Teas including Purple, Yellow, Green, White, Silver Needles, and Oolong.

Operations Manager

Brings over a decade of experience across tea production and auction operations. Oversees end-to-end auction activities, stakeholder engagement, and ensures value delivery to producers, buyers, warehouses, and industry partners.

Tea Executive – Black CTC

Over 18 years in the Mombasa tea trade. Specializes in auction operations, catalogue closing, tea tasting, reporting, marketing, buyer relations, and warehouse coordination.

Accountant

CPA-qualified accountant with extensive experience in finance and accounting within the tea and beverage sectors. Oversees accounts, reconciliations, catalogue access, and financial reporting while supporting buyer and warehouse relations.

Tea Executive – Orthodox & Specialty Tea

Focuses on Orthodox and Specialty tea segments. Provides technical and market expertise to producers and buyers, while supporting regular Black CTC auction activities. Skilled in data analysis, reporting, and specialty tea marketing.

Tea Executive Assistant / Documentation

Supports auction operations with catalogue preparation, documentation, iTTS access, tea tasting reports, and smooth coordination between producers, buyers, and warehouses.

Supervisor – Tea Tasting, Sampling & Distribution

Expert in tea sampling, brewing, and liquoring. Manages tasting sessions, distribution of producer samples, and ensures timely coordination with buyers and warehouses.
